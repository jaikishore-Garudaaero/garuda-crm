Garuda Aerospace Academy · RPTO

Become a DGCA-certified drone pilot

DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training Organisation (Authorisation No. 23/2022). Ground theory, simulator, assembly and real flying, with batches every month.

  • Centres in Chennai, Jaipur, Madhya Pradesh and Assam, or on your campus where approved
  • DGCA type-certified training drones, AV classrooms, simulators, airfield and maintenance bay
  • Drone and third-party insurance, safety induction before every flight
  • Remote Pilot Certificate issued by DGCA on the DigitalSky platform
