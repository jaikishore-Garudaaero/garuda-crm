Before you call

Straight answers

Who can become a drone pilot?

Anyone aged 18–65 who has passed 10th standard can take the Small or Medium Class RPC. Train the Trainer needs an RPC and a degree.

How long does the course take, and who issues the certificate?

Six days: two days of ground theory, a day of tests and simulator, then three days of flying and evaluation. On passing, DGCA issues the Remote Pilot Certificate on the DigitalSky platform.

Where are the training centres?

Chennai, Jaipur, Madhya Pradesh and Assam. For colleges, training can also run on campus where approved.

Can our college set up a drone lab or CoE?

Yes. Choose a Starter Lab, an Advanced Lab or a full Drone CoE. Every lab includes an agri drone, Droni drones, DIY kits and simulators, plus faculty training. Lab programmes are shared 50:50, so workshops and spraying services help pay the lab back.

Can I buy a Kisan drone with a subsidy or loan?

Garuda is empanelled for the SMAM scheme in 16 states, supports Namo Drone Didi, and was the first drone company to offer loans through public sector banks under the Agri Infra Fund. Our team will check what you're eligible for.

What do courses and labs cost?

Fees depend on the course, batch size and location, and lab prices on the package. Call or WhatsApp us for the current fee sheet and a proposal.
